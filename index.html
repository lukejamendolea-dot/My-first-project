Cooked Salted Eggs

Begin with the first step

Ingredients

  • 3 cracked eggs
  • One Plastic Bowl
  • Hot Pink salt"
  • One Metal Pan

Instructions

  1. One Metal Pan
  2. 3 Cracked Eggs
  3. One Plastic Bowl
  4. Hot Pink Salt

Grab 3 eggs and crack them, put them in a bowl and throw away the shells. Make sure to mix the eggs in the bowl and then pour them on the pan. Let the eggs start to cook and boil until it bubbles a little bit and then pull the eggs close together until it begins to harden. Begin to flip the eggs on both sides until it feels cooked and pour it on your plate that's empty.
